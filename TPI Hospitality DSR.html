Section 02 / Business Case + Proposed Solution

The Problem You're Solving — and How Reeco Closes It.

Three pain points. Three direct answers.

01
The Problem
Invoices That Eat Your Team's Time

Every property is manually coding invoices — an error-prone, time-consuming process that grows harder to manage as the portfolio expands. Line-item coding that should be automatic becomes a daily burden on your accounting staff and property-level managers alike.

The Answer
AP That Codes Itself

Reeco's AP automation captures every invoice — paper or email — and codes it by line item using machine learning trained to your GL structure. What used to take 7–10 minutes per invoice takes about 20 seconds. The system learns your coding patterns over time and improves continuously.

02
The Problem
No Unified View of What the Portfolio Is Spending

Purchasing decisions happen property by property, with no centralized visibility. Spend variances only surface at month-end, too late to act. GMs have no real-time signal about where they stand against budget before they submit a purchase order.

The Answer
A Live Checkbook at Every Property

Reeco's Profit Sword integration pushes daily PO spend data into your existing budget system and pulls GL and forecast data back. GMs see their budget position inside the ordering workflow — before they click submit. Variances get caught at the PO, not at month-end review.

03
The Problem
Supplier Management Without Centralized IT Support

With properties spread across Minnesota and Florida, managing supplier catalog integrations — HSM, broadliners, local vendors — requires centralized oversight that your properties simply don't have. Each hotel is managing its own relationships without a common system or pricing visibility.

The Answer
One Curated Store. Reeco Manages the Integrations.

Reeco builds and maintains your supplier catalog — HSM items, broadliner pricing, and offline vendors — all in one store. Properties don't interact with supplier portals or manage credentials. Reeco's team handles all supplier onboarding, integration setup, and troubleshooting via a support ticket system.

Section 03 / The Platform

What's Included.

Four modules. One login. Built for hospitality back-of-house from the ground up.

Purchasing & Receiving

Full visibility and control across F&B, housekeeping, engineering, and service agreements. All your suppliers in one connected place — existing partnerships, pricing, and agreements honored. Mobile receiving lets your team check in orders on the spot; Reeco handles missing items and credits automatically.

AP Automation

Every invoice — organized, AI-coded by line item, and ready to sync with your accounting system. What used to take 7–10 minutes per invoice takes about 20 seconds. The coding learns over time, so it gets faster and more accurate the longer you use it.

Section 07 / FAQs

Questions We Expect — Answered Directly.

If something's missing, email Zander and we'll add it before the meeting.

"We've never rolled out a procurement platform portfolio-wide before. How do you manage that?"
Reeco doesn't require a mandated top-down rollout. The most successful implementations start with a pilot — at least five properties, driven by managers who become advocates. From there, GM-to-GM peer influence does the work. We've supported similar pilot-first strategies at multi-property portfolios across the country. A full rollout plan specific to TPI will be presented at the proposal meeting.
"What about Dynamics GP? We haven't heard of other procurement platforms integrating with it."
That's correct — we're building the Dynamics GP integration specifically in the context of TPI. Paul Bennie confirmed on our June 9 call that this integration is included in scope at no additional charge to TPI. Reeco handles the build and configuration; there is no fee on the Microsoft GP side for API access.
"Will the Profit Sword integration actually give our GMs real-time budget visibility?"
Yes. Profit Sword integration is two-way and live. Reeco pushes daily PO spend to Profit Sword, and budget and GL forecast data flows back into the Reeco ordering workflow. GMs see their in-month forecast before they submit a purchase order — the same checkbook behavior your finance team already uses in Profit Sword, surfaced at the moment of ordering.
"What about Margaritaville? It's a different operation from our branded hotels."
We're transparent about this. Margaritaville Fort Myers Beach has a centralized requisition process that Reeco doesn't fully support today. This feature is actively on our roadmap. Zander will provide a specific roadmap update with a confirmed timeline before any Margaritaville go-live is discussed. For now, the rest of the TPI portfolio — including Compass Naples, all select-service properties, and your full-service branded hotels — is fully supported today.
"How does security and data compliance work?"
Reeco is SOC 2 Type II certified. We do not store payment card data. Property credentials are handled through secure onboarding screens, and your accounting system credentials are never shared across properties. Full SOC 2 report available upon request — email zanders@reeco.com.
